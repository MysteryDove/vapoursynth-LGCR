# Optional BM Refinement Study Protocol

This protocol was frozen before `evaluation/bm_study.py` was run. The study is
independent of the paper's frozen single-frame benchmark and does not amend its
claims or selected methods.

## Question

For otherwise identical `Recon` and `TRecon` calls, how much does enabling the
optional `bm` collaborative stage change reconstruction error and boundary
artifacts?

All reported effects are paired `bm=True` minus `bm=False` differences. A
negative error delta favors BM. Transition-spread delta remains a signed
descriptor rather than an improvement endpoint.

## Recon Design

- Scenes: 64 new deterministic scenes, seeds 3000-3063, generated by
  `evaluation.common.make_scene` with split label `bm_holdout`.
- Degradation kernels: box, triangle, bicubic, and Lanczos.
- True and assumed siting: matched left and center.
- Observation conditions:
  - `clean`: floating-point luma/chroma after subsampling;
  - `q10`: the same observation quantized to 10 bits;
  - `q10_noise`: 10-bit observation plus deterministic independent Gaussian
    noise with sigma 0.006 on each low-resolution chroma plane, clipped to
    [-0.5, 0.5].
- Frozen reconstruction bases:
  - default algo 2 with Lanczos3;
  - paper algo 6 with Jinc3;
  - supplemental algo 6 with Lanczos4.
- Other parameters: strength 0.8, sparse 0, ar 0, and public defaults.

The primary Recon endpoint is scene-weighted edge MAE within each observation
condition and reconstruction base. Secondary endpoints are full-frame MAE,
PSNR, smooth-region MAE, bleed-profile error, phase displacement, tangent
alias, transition-spread delta, and ringing mass.

## TRecon Design

- Scenes: 32 new deterministic center frames, seeds 4000-4031.
- Three-frame clips are constructed by integer horizontal translation of the
  complete source scene by `-motion`, 0, and `+motion` pixels.
- Motion magnitudes: 0, 1, 2, and 3 luma pixels per adjacent frame.
- Degradation: triangle, correctly interpreted left siting.
- Observation conditions: `clean` and `q10_noise`, defined as above with
  independent noise per frame.
- TRecon parameters: strength 0.8, temporal radius 1, and other public
  defaults. Only `bm` changes within each pair.

The primary TRecon endpoint is edge MAE by observation condition and motion.
The same secondary metrics as Recon are retained.

## Statistics And Reporting

- The independent unit is the generated scene, not a degradation, siting,
  frame, or pixel.
- Paired scene means are bootstrapped with 4,000 replicates and seed 20260811.
- Reports include mean off/on values, paired deltas with 95% intervals, the
  fraction of scenes improved in edge MAE, degradation/condition strata, and
  the five largest edge-MAE regressions.
- Raw rows are written to `evaluation/results/bm_study.csv`; the generated
  report is `evaluation/results/bm_study.md`.
- This synthetic study does not establish subjective benefit or real-video
  prevalence. The noise condition is a controlled stressor, not a codec model.
